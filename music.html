2026

Fantasie-Impromptu is one of the famous solo piano pieces of the Romantic Era. It is well known for its tenary structure (A-B-A), where it starts and ends with a restless, stormy melody, while containing a lyrical, flowing middle part.

This is an orchastrated version of "Summer" by the Japanese composer Joe Hisaishi. I along with my friends from the Orchestra played this piece to help fundraise for the Texas Center for the Missing gala.

2025

Chopin's Étude Op. 10, No.5 in G-flat major is popularly known as the "Black Key Etude" because the right hand plays a continous stream of notes only on the black keys (with one white note hidden throughout the entire work). Published in 1833, this piece is a frequently used technical study to help with finger agility.

2023-2024

Pierette, Air de Ballet was composed during the late Romantic period and is known for its charming, lighthearted character. "Air de Ballet" describes a graceful, light piece designed to evoke the movement of a ballet scene.

Allegro by Mozart (The 1st movement of Piano Sonata No.12 in F Major) is written in 3/4 time. The first theme is in F major, then transitions between D minor and C minor. Finally, the key ends in a sparkling C major coda.

Paul's Favorite Music Sites:


Hooktheory is an amazing site that I use to research the chord progressions of tens of thousands of music. It's also a place where I can compose some of my own music!


Rhapsodate is a site which helps me a lot regarding chamber music. If I'm playing a piece with the Viking Orchestra, I use this to explore the roles of different instruments and some background information about the music.


IMSLP is a public domain that contains around 15 million pages of music and related documents. I use this source to look for new music, and learn more about different composers.
